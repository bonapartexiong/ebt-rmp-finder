🍽️ CalFresh RMP Restaurant Finder

Find hot-meal restaurants near any California ZIP code that accept EBT through the Restaurant Meals Program.

Who can use these restaurants? CalFresh households where every member is 60 or older, has a disability, or is experiencing homelessness (spouses included). Only approved locations can take EBT for hot, prepared food — this finder lists the federally-approved vendors.
Official sources
CDSS CalFresh Restaurant Meals Program · CDSS statewide restaurant list (spreadsheet) · California EBT locator map
Vendor data: USDA Food and Nutrition Service SNAP retailer database, queried live. Business hours are not published in the public vendor dataset — tap a marker and follow the directions link to check hours.
Built for California. RMP eligibility: all household members must be 60+, disabled, or homeless (spouses excepted from the criteria). Participation is per restaurant location, not per chain. If a location stops accepting EBT, look for the official RMP decal in the window or confirm with the county office. Map © OpenStreetMap contributors.
