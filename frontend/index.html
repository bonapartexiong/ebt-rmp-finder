🍽️ CalFresh RMP Restaurant Finder

Find hot-meal restaurants near any California ZIP code that accept EBT through the Restaurant Meals Program.

Who can use these restaurants? CalFresh households where every member is 60 or older, has a disability, or is experiencing homelessness (spouses included). Only approved locations can take EBT for hot, prepared food — this finder lists the federally-approved vendors.

Vendor data: USDA Food and Nutrition Service SNAP retailer database, queried live. Chain menus/prices: typical California menu prices, compiled Oct 2026 — verify in store.
Built for California. RMP eligibility: all household members must be 60+, disabled, or homeless (spouses excepted from the criteria).
